Afrikaans and isiXhosa lesson translations are pilot/draft translations until reviewed by suitable fluent/native speakers. Software compilation or CI success is not linguistic certification.

## Scope
Review all eight canonical Level 1 lessons in Afrikaans and isiXhosa against the English source while preserving the same lesson IDs, quiz structure and correct answers.

## Review criteria
For each lesson confirm:
- meaning matches the English learning purpose;
- wording is natural and understandable to an ordinary first-time AI learner;
- technical language follows the plain-language-first rule;
- South African examples and social context remain appropriate;
- tone is respectful and not unnecessarily academic;
- THINK behaviour remains clear: try, understand, check, work independently, explain learning;
- safety, privacy, fairness and human-responsibility meaning has not weakened;
- quiz question meaning is preserved and the existing correct answer remains correct;
- no translation accidentally changes assessment difficulty or creates ambiguity.

## Recording
Record reviewer name/role only with their permission, language reviewed, date, lesson number, PASS/CHANGE NEEDED, and the exact proposed wording for any correction.

Do not change canonical IDs, progress keys, quiz correctIndex values or curriculum structure during language correction.
